# Frequently asked questions

## Do you accept walk-ins?
No. We see patients by appointment only.

## Do you offer telehealth?
Yes. We offer telehealth video visits for follow-ups.

## Are you accepting new patients?
No, we are not accepting new patients right now.

## How long do prescription refills take?
Prescription refills are processed within 2 business days.

## Are you open on Saturdays?
We are closed on Saturdays.

## How do I reach you?
Call our main line at (555) 555-0160.

## Where do I park?
Free parking is available in the lot next to the building.

## What should I bring to my first visit?
Please bring a photo ID, your insurance card and a list of your medications.
