# Red-flag protocols
Each rule: symptom | keywords | escalation | what to say

- fainting | keywords: fainted, passed out, fainting | escalation: emergency | say: Please hang up and call 911 now.
- shortness of breath | keywords: shortness of breath, can't breathe, cannot breathe, trouble breathing | escalation: emergency | say: Please hang up and call 911 now.
- palpitations | keywords: racing heart, palpitations, heart racing | escalation: urgent | say: I'm connecting you to the nurse line now.
- leg swelling | keywords: swollen leg, leg swelling | escalation: urgent | say: I'm connecting you to the nurse line now.
- severe headache | keywords: worst headache, severe headache | escalation: emergency | say: Please hang up and call 911 now.
- confusion | keywords: confused, confusion | escalation: emergency | say: Please hang up and call 911 now.
