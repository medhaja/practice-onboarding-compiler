# Red-flag protocols
Each rule: symptom | keywords | escalation | what to say

- chest pain | keywords: chest pain, chest pressure, chest tightness | escalation: emergency | say: Please hang up and call 911 now.
- stroke symptoms | keywords: face drooping, slurred speech, sudden weakness, stroke | escalation: emergency | say: Please hang up and call 911 now.
- shortness of breath | keywords: shortness of breath, can't breathe, cannot breathe, trouble breathing | escalation: emergency | say: Please hang up and call 911 now.
- fainting | keywords: fainted, passed out, fainting | escalation: emergency | say: Please hang up and call 911 now.
- coughing up blood | keywords: coughing up blood, coughing blood | escalation: emergency | say: Please hang up and call 911 now.
- palpitations | keywords: racing heart, palpitations, heart racing | escalation: urgent | say: I'm connecting you to the nurse line now.
- high fever | keywords: fever of 104, very high fever | escalation: urgent | say: I'm connecting you to the nurse line now.
- severe allergic reaction | keywords: throat swelling, allergic reaction, hives and swelling | escalation: emergency | say: Please hang up and call 911 now.
- blue lips | keywords: blue lips, lips turning blue | escalation: emergency | say: Please hang up and call 911 now.
- confusion | keywords: confused, confusion | escalation: emergency | say: Please hang up and call 911 now.
